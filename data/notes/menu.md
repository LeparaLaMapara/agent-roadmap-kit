# Menu

All prices are in rand and include a cooldrink when you order a full plate.

## Plates

- Full plate: pap, beef stew, chakalaka and spinach. R85.
- Chicken plate: pap or rice, braised chicken, beetroot salad. R80.
- Vegetarian plate: rice, bean curry, chakalaka and pumpkin. R70.
- Kota: quarter loaf with chips, polony, cheese and atchar. R45.

## Sunday specials

On Sundays the kitchen cooks a traditional lunch of rice, roast chicken, seven colours and custard for dessert. R110 a plate. Sunday lunch must be ordered by Saturday at 6pm.

## Extras

- Extra chakalaka: R15.
- Vetkoek with mince: R25.
- Two litre cooldrink: R30.
