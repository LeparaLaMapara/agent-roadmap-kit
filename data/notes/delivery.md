# Delivery

The kitchen delivers to Soshanguve blocks F, G, H, L and M, and to Soshanguve South. Delivery costs R20 per order.

Orders for Mabopane and Winterveldt are collection only. You can collect from the house in Block L, next to the Shell garage on Ruth First Road.

Deliveries go out between 11am and 7pm. Orders placed after 6pm are delivered the next day.

If the driver cannot reach you within ten minutes of arriving, the order goes back to the kitchen and can be collected until 8pm.
