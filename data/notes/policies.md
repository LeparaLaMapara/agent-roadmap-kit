# Policies

## Allergies

The kitchen uses peanuts in the Sunday seven colours and wheat in the vetkoek and kota. Tell us about any allergy when you order. We cannot promise that any dish is completely free of peanuts because everything is cooked in one kitchen.

## Refunds

If an order is wrong or arrives cold, send a photo on WhatsApp within one hour and we will replace it or refund you in full. We do not refund orders that the driver could not deliver because nobody answered.
