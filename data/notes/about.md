# About Mama Dineo's Kitchen

Mama Dineo's Kitchen is a home kitchen in Soshanguve, north of Pretoria, run by Dineo Mokoena and her two daughters. It started in 2019 cooking for neighbours on weekends and now takes orders six days a week.

This is a sample business made up for the Agent Roadmap Kit. The notes in this folder are the kind of documents a small business really has: a menu, delivery rules, payment options, and a few private notes that should never reach a customer.

The kitchen cooks everything fresh on the day. Nothing is frozen and reheated.
