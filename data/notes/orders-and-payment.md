# Orders and payment

Place orders on WhatsApp. Send your name, your block and street, what you want, and whether you want delivery or collection.

We accept cash on delivery, EFT, and SnapScan. For EFT, use your name and block as the reference and send proof of payment on WhatsApp before the order is cooked.

Orders of more than ten plates, for funerals, parties or church events, need two days notice and a 50% deposit.
